Signature moments — experiments
Confirmed A and B worked because they arrive after something real happened and they speak in the second person. This page pushes that same voice to the other places in the journey where something changes hands: a slot, a link, an address, a page going live, a first booking, a review. Same rules — plum and full-bleed are each spent once per journey; the rest is white paper and typography. Each frame is tagged MVP (data exists today) or DREAM (needs something you'd have to build).

Where the voice goes, and where it doesn't
Every frame here sits at a hand-over: a slot held, a link honoured, an address released, a review published, a page made public, a first booking landed, a refund sent. Forms, lists and settings stay quiet — the letter only works if it's rare. Budget per journey: customer spends full-bleed on Confirmed and never plum; provider spends plum on Published and never full-bleed. 01 and 02 are the sign-in step rewritten so the hold is the subject, not the form. 03 is the booking detail on the day itself: the address becomes the headline because that's the one thing she needs at 11:20 outside on Wilmslow Road. 04 renders the review as it will appear on the page, which is also an honest reminder that it can't be taken back. 08 is the only dream: it needs an evening-before email or a scheduled job, which the docs say doesn't exist. 09 is your empty-search Discover as a masthead — counts by category come from the same query the results page runs.
