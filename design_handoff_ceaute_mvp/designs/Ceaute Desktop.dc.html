How to read the pairs
Provider page: A is a listing, B is a gallery — pick by whether the first desktop second should sell the work or the price. Booking: A keeps a URL per step and a persistent summary, B keeps the provider in view — pick by how much you trust modals in a web app (I don't, much). Dashboard: A is a report with a diary in it, B is a diary with a report in the corner — ask your sister which one she'd open at 8am. Discover, catalogue editor and customer bookings are single-direction follow-ups once these three are decided; they inherit the winner's frame.
